Methodology

Load calculated using sRPE (session RPE): duration × RPE. RPE derived from intensity percentage (intensity% ÷ 10). All benchmarks are from single-team monitoring studies — your total cross-team load has no direct published comparator, which is the point. Age-based threshold from Jayanthi et al. (2013) and NATA position statement. Published benchmarks: Cruz et al. 2018 (PeerJ); Palmer et al. 2023 (Sports); Lever et al. 2024 (JSCR); Lever et al. 2024 (J Sports Sci); Athletics Australia guidelines. This tool is for educational purposes and does not replace clinical assessment.
